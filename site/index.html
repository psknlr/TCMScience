Abstract摘要

Chinese medicine rests on evidence of very different kinds: a Shanghan Lun passage, a pharmacopoeia entry, a docking score, a cell assay, a randomized trial. A language-model agent that treats them all as "evidence text" will, sooner or later, present a classical record as a clinical finding or a network prediction as a mechanism. TCMScience makes that failure impossible to express rather than merely discouraged. The model plans and reasons; a separate trusted kernel decides what it may run, labels every datum at entry, and releases a scientific claim only when the evidence behind it licenses that kind of claim. Every dataset the agent reads is a content-hashed snapshot recorded in a hash-chained ledger, so each result names the exact data it came from. Applied to the classical formula 葛根芩连汤, the system reproduces the standard network-pharmacology result and then shows it to be an artefact of which proteins had been assayed. What survives is a cytochrome P450 inhibition signal that bears on herb–drug interactions rather than on efficacy.

中医药的证据种类差异极大：一段《伤寒论》条文、一条药典记载、一个对接分数、一次细胞实验、一项随机对照试验。把它们一律当作「证据文本」的语言模型智能体，迟早会把古籍记载说成临床发现，把网络预测说成作用机制。TCMScience 的做法是让这种错误无法表达，而不是靠提示词劝阻。模型负责规划和推理；独立的可信内核决定它能运行什么，在入口给每条数据打标签，并且只有当证据足以支撑某类主张时，才放行这条科学主张。智能体读取的每个数据集都是内容哈希快照，记录在哈希链账本中，因此每个结果都能指明它来自哪一份数据。用于经典方剂葛根芩连汤时，系统复现了常见的网络药理学结论，随后证明它是「哪些蛋白被检测过」造成的假象。剩下的是一个细胞色素 P450 抑制信号：它关乎中药—药物相互作用，而不是疗效。

Contributions主要贡献

Four things that are checked, not promised四条可以逐条核对的主张

Each is enforced by code and covered by tests; none depends on the model behaving.每一条都由代码强制执行、由测试覆盖，不依赖模型「守规矩」。

01

Evidence kinds are types, not grades证据种类是类型，不是等级

The study design is stored; the tier is derived from it.存储的是研究设计，证据等级由它推导。

Prevents a classical record being read as clinical evidence.防止把古籍记载当成临床证据。

02

A prediction cannot become a fact计算预测无法变成事实

Predictive designs license a mechanism hypothesis and nothing stronger, in the kernel's own table.在内核的主张—支撑表中，预测性设计只能支撑机制假说。

Prevents network pharmacology reported as "the mechanism".防止把网络药理学写成「作用机制」。

03

Data enter as audited snapshots数据以可审计快照进入

Parse, gate, hash, ledger — each source under a card with its licence and access terms.解析、质量门、哈希、账本；每个数据源都有写明许可与访问条件的卡片。

Prevents results that cannot name their data.防止结果说不清来自哪份数据。

04

Independent version axes独立的版本轴

Skills, sources and benchmarks version separately; a monthly update may discover a skill, never promote it.Skill、数据源、基准各自版本化；月度更新能发现 Skill，不能让它上线。

Prevents incomparable scores and unreviewed skills going live.防止分数不可比、未审查的 Skill 上线。

Finding 2

With inactive results included, one signal remains纳入阴性结果后，只剩一个信号

PubChem BioAssay adds 143,702 results — 10,710 active and 132,992 inactive. On 116 proteins tested against ≥ 20 constituents (12,579 tests, 8.6 % active), the pathways that pass are cytochrome P450 pathways (q = 0.023–0.026, 10,000 permutations), as at ≥ 50 constituents (q = 0.017). They rest on CYP1A2 (136 of 205 constituents active) and CYP2C9 (78 of 206) in uniform Tox21 panels.PubChem BioAssay 提供 143,702 条检测结果，其中 10,710 条有活性、132,992 条无活性。在测过 ≥ 20 个成分的 116 个蛋白上（12,579 次检测，8.6% 有活性），通过检验的是细胞色素 P450 通路（10,000 次置换，q = 0.023–0.026）；阈值为 ≥ 50 时同样如此（q = 0.017）。信号来自 Tox21 等统一面板中的 CYP1A2（205 个成分中 136 个有活性）和 CYP2C9（206 个中 78 个）。

This is a herb–drug interaction signal, not evidence for a mechanism of action. At the loosest threshold (≥ 10) a carbonic-anhydrase pathway passes instead, carried by small literature sets chosen for their actives.这是中药—药物相互作用信号，不是作用机制的证据。阈值放宽到 ≥ 10 时，通过的变成碳酸酐酶通路，那是少量按阳性挑选的文献检测造成的。

Method方法

Which evidence licenses which claim什么证据能支撑什么主张

The figure is drawn from the table the kernel enforces, so it cannot disagree with the code.这张图直接由内核强制执行的表生成，因此不可能与代码不一致。

At compile time编译时

A skill whose steps cannot license its declared claim kind is rejected (EVIDENCE103).步骤支撑不了所声明主张类型的 Skill 被拒绝（EVIDENCE103）。

At the claim主张检查时

Every cited item must license the claim kind — the weakest link decides (CLM005).引用的每一条证据都必须能支撑该主张，由最弱一环决定（CLM005）。

At release发布前

Every edge of the claim's path must exist in a verified snapshot; the strongest kind the path supports is recorded with the reason it stops there.主张路径上的每条边都必须存在于已校验的快照中；同时记录该路径最多能支撑哪类主张，以及为什么停在这里。

Skills and sourcesSkill 与数据源

Every skill states what it refuses to do. Every source has a card; web access is off unless a person approved it, and a skill can narrow its sources but never widen them.每个 Skill 都写明它拒绝做什么。每个数据源都有卡片；网页访问默认关闭、需人工批准，Skill 只能缩小可用数据源，不能扩大。

TCMScience Arena

A read-only evaluation site: six tracks, eight dimensions always shown decomposed, and runs stopped by a hard gate kept visible. It renders results and never computes a score, so changing the site cannot change a result.只读评测站点：六个赛道，八个维度始终分项展示，被硬门槛拦下的运行仍然可见。它只渲染结果、从不计算分数，所以改动站点也改不了结果。

What we do not claim我们不声称什么

Stated limits明确的边界

  • No general immunity to prompt injection, no certification-grade de-identification, no tamper-proof execution environment.不声称对提示注入有通用免疫力、不声称认证级去标识化、不声称执行环境不可篡改。
  • In-process components are not isolated; the bundled sandbox backend is a no-op and says so.进程内组件没有隔离；随包的沙箱后端是空实现，并且它自己这么说。
  • The audit chain is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.审计链能察觉篡改，不能防止篡改。
  • Every analytic result carries its limitations; not significant is not irrelevant.每个分析结果都附有局限说明；「不显著」不等于「无关」。
