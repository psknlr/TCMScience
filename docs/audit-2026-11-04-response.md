# Response to the tool and database availability audit of 4 November 2026

The audit (`TCMScience_Tool_and_Database_Availability_Audit_20261104.xlsx`, nine worksheets) called all 444
registered connector operations through the production gateway at science.impf.ai. Of the 24 that failed on the
first attempt, 5 recovered on retry and 19 failed again across eight sources. Two defects let the system report
success without usable research evidence. This note maps each priority finding to the change that answers it, the
test that holds it, and what is still open. The workbook is not committed; the operation-level observations it
records are, as `studio/runner/src/tcmstudio/data/operational_status.json`.

## What changed

| Priority | Finding | Change | Acceptance test |
|---|---|---|---|

The dispatcher reports such an answer as `error.type: invalid_response`. | `BioScience-Harness/tests/test_response_integrity.py`; `studio/runner/tests/test_audit_20261104.py` (the audit's replay; the four-accession positive control; challenge page, malformed and empty payloads). |
| **P0** | Requesting LOTUS in a hub holding NPASS and CMAUP, or any store of an empty hub, returned `succeeded` with zero rows. | `TCMDataHub.coverage`, `require` and `search_relations` report the stores requested, searched and missing (with the step that builds each). `relations()` refuses a query whose every requested store is missing (`DatasetNotBuilt`); an unknown dataset key or relation kind is an input error first. A missing store is never a silent source in consensus. ## Still open

- **The paginated UniProt route in the browser.** The browser builds snapshots from files the production relay
  downloads (`studio/edge/src/datasets.js`); the relay still serves only the `stream` URL. The fallback runs on the
  runner and in local runs. Bringing it to the relay needs the gateway registry to admit the paged `search` template.
- **Reviewed digests** for `uniprot_human_reviewed_accessions.txt` (per release), `9606.protein.aliases.v12.0.txt.gz`
  and `UniProt2Reactome.txt`, so snapshot integrity can be `PASS` rather than `UNVERIFIED`.
- **HERB and DCABM-TCM.** The status record now shows them failing, by layer, with their last success; their
  endpoints, authorization and a verifiable snapshot still need checking, and the gateway has no circuit breaker.
- **Runner dependencies** (docking, ADMET, transcriptomics) blocked three research scenarios; physical GPUs and
  user-configured Runner environments were outside the audit.
- **P2 latency** of a named tool (two catalog searches before `native.gc_content`), and the model's incorrect claim
  that the GC tool does not validate its input, are not addressed here.
- **The status record is a snapshot.** It changes only by a reviewed commit (`operational_status.py
  --audit-workbook` or `--live-run`); the scheduled workflow does not write it yet.
